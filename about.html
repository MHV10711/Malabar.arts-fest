PROFILE

Creativity,
organised digitally.

MIS Art Fest is a celebration of creativity, talent and participation. This platform was developed to give that experience a clear and accessible digital presence.

From presenting programs and participants to displaying results and the overall championship, the aim was simple: make the Art Fest easier to explore, follow and experience.
